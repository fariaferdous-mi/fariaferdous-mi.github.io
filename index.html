About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh. My research lies in experimental condensed matter physics and materials science, with particular emphasis on magnetic and multiferroic materials, ferrite nanomaterials, functional oxide thin films, photocatalytic nanocomposites, and solar-cell transport layers.

My research focuses on materials synthesis, structural and optical characterization, magnetic and electrical property analysis, and understanding structure-property relationships relevant to energy, electronic, and environmental applications.

I am interested in collaborations involving functional materials, thin films, magnetic materials, spin-dependent phenomena, nanocomposites, and energy materials.
