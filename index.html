About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh. My research lies at the intersection of experimental condensed matter physics and materials science, with particular interests in magnetic and multiferroic materials, functional oxides, ferrite nanomaterials, thin films, and energy-related materials. My work focuses on understanding how composition, crystal structure, microstructure, defects, interfaces, and processing conditions influence the magnetic, electrical, dielectric, optical, and functional properties of materials.

My research experience spans multiferroic composites, ferrite nanoparticles, photocatalytic nanocomposites, and metal-oxide thin films. I am particularly interested in developing functional materials and heterostructures for magnetic, electronic, energy-conversion, and environmental applications.

Current Research Projects

Graphene-Oxide-Based Semiconductor Nanocomposites
Principal Investigator · University Grants Commission (UGC), Bangladesh · 2025–2026

Investigation of graphene-oxide-based semiconducting nanocomposites for ultrafast photocatalytic degradation in wastewater management.

Metal-Oxide Thin Films for Perovskite Solar Cells

Fabrication of metal-oxide thin films using chemical bath deposition for efficient electron transport layers in perovskite solar cells.

Ferrite/CeO₂ Heterojunction Nanocomposites

Investigation of the structural, magnetic, dielectric, optical, and photocatalytic properties of Cu-substituted manganese ferrite/CeO₂ heterojunction nanocomposites.

NiO-Based Hole Transport Layers

Development of calcium-doped NiO hole transport layers using spin coating for solar-cell applications.
