About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh. My research interests lie in experimental condensed matter physics and materials science, particularly in understanding how composition, microstructure, defects, interfaces, and processing conditions influence the physical properties of functional materials.

My research experience includes magnetic and multiferroic materials, ferrite nanomaterials, functional oxides, semiconductor nanocomposites, and thin-film materials. I am particularly interested in developing structure-property relationships that can guide the design of materials for magnetic, electronic, spintronic, and energy-related applications.

Current Research

I currently serve as Principal Investigator of a research project supported by the University Grants Commission of Bangladesh, investigating graphene-oxide-based semiconducting nanocomposites for enhanced photocatalytic degradation and wastewater-management applications.
