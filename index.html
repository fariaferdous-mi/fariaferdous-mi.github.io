About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh. My research interests lie in experimental condensed matter physics and materials science, with particular emphasis on magnetic and multiferroic materials, ferrite nanomaterials, functional oxide thin films, photocatalytic nanocomposites, and solar-cell transport layers.

My research encompasses materials synthesis, structural and optical characterization, magnetic and electrical property analysis, and the investigation of structure–property relationships. I am particularly interested in understanding how composition, microstructure, interfaces, and processing conditions govern the functional properties of materials for electronic, energy, and environmental applications.

Recent

  • 2025–2026: Principal Investigator of a University Grants Commission (UGC), Bangladesh research project investigating graphene-oxide-based semiconducting nanocomposites for ultrafast photocatalytic degradation in wastewater management.
  • 2025: Published research on the photocatalytic performance and recyclability of ZnO–rGO nanocomposites for wastewater treatment in AIP Advances.
  • Current research includes metal-oxide thin films for perovskite solar cells, ferrite/CeO₂ heterojunction nanocomposites, and NiO-based hole transport layers.
